YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL

Privacy & data.

QUIT PROOF is designed to keep your personal entries on this browser.

Local-first storage

ON THIS DEVICE

Your answers, plan, and proof log are stored in this browser's local storage. This prototype does not send them to a server or require an account. Anyone with access to this browser profile or device may still be able to see them.

Good to know: clearing browser data, using private browsing, or changing devices may erase or separate your entries. Export a backup if you want to keep a copy. Avoid entering sensitive information you don't need.

Health and support note

This tool is for self-reflection and planning. It does not diagnose nicotine dependence, provide medical care, or guarantee quitting success. Behavioral support can help people quit. Evidence-based treatments, including medication, can improve the chances of success; choosing whether to use them is a personal decision to discuss with a qualified healthcare professional. If you're in immediate distress or have concerning symptoms, seek appropriate medical help.

If you're in the United States, CDC resources are available at cdc.gov/tobacco. Other countries have local cessation services.
